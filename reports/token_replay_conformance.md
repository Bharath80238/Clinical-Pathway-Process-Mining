## 2. Mathematical Formalism of Token-Based Replay

Token-based replay evaluates conformance by firing transitions corresponding to observed activities in the trace $\sigma = \langle a_1, a_2, \dots, a_k \rangle$:

1. **Initial Marking ($M_i$)**: A single initial token is deposited into the source place `source_place`.
2. **Transition Firing Rules**:
   - For an activity $a_i$ to fire, all input places $\bullet t_{a_i}$ must possess at least one token.
   - If a required token is absent, a **missing token** ($m$) is artificially generated to allow the replay to proceed.
   - Firing transition $t_{a_i}$ consumes tokens from $\bullet t_{a_i}$ and produces tokens into output places $t_{a_i} \bullet$.
3. **Final Marking ($M_f$)**: Upon trace completion, tokens are evaluated at the target sink place `sink_place`. Any tokens left in other places are recorded as **remaining tokens** ($r$).

The per-trace replay fitness score is calculated via:

$$fitness(\sigma, N) = \frac{1}{2}\left(1 - \frac{m}{c}\right) + \frac{1}{2}\left(1 - \frac{r}{p}\right)$$

Where:
- $p$: Total tokens produced during replay
- $c$: Total tokens consumed during replay
- $m$: Total missing tokens forced during replay
- $r$: Total residual tokens remaining after reaching the sink
